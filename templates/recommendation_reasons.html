Why We Recommend These Books?

Understand how our recommendation algorithm selects similar books based on your favorites

Recommendation Principles

Our recommendation system uses two main methods:

  • Quick Recommendations (MF Method): Uses Matrix Factorization to identify patterns in users' book ratings and preferences
  • Accurate Recommendations (Hybrid Method): Combines content-based and collaborative filtering for more precise recommendations

The similarity factors we consider include:

  • Title Similarity: Books with titles similar to your favorites receive higher matching scores
  • Author Matching: Other works by the same author are more likely to match your reading preferences
  • Rating Patterns: Users with similar reading patterns to yours often enjoy similar books
  • Combined Score: We combine these factors to calculate the final similarity score

Similarity scores range from 0 to 1, with higher scores indicating greater similarity to your favorite books.
